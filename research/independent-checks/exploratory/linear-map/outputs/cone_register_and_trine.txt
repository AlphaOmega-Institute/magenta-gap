A. Injectivity and diagonality
   smallest singular value of A: 9.699e-05 (largest 1.360e-01); A*A is diagonal with entries a_L+a_M+a_S, smallest 9.406e-09 at 830 nm
   pulled-back cone projections equal diag(a_i); largest off-diagonal entry of any pulled-back observable: 0.0e+00
B. Positivity
   cone channels are orthogonal projections on the three-level register, hence positive.
   lights with tabulated S = 0: 616-830 nm. L/(L+M) at 616 nm = 0.80654, at 700 nm = 0.94167.
   A nonzero positive operator on two levels has a kernel of dimension at most one, so two S = 0 lights would
   have to share one colour state and one L:M ratio. They do not, so the S channel cannot be a positive
   operator on a two-level factor alone.
C. Reduced two-level state of each light

   opponent formula: x = a_L - (a_M + a_S)/2,  y = (sqrt(3)/2)(a_M - a_S),  both divided by a_L + a_M + a_S
   equal-energy white: Bloch vector (-1.5e-16, -3.0e-16), i.e. the centre (maximally mixed)
